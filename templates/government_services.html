Why isn't the API connected yet?

Official BHASHINI access involves organization registration, review and approval. We do not request or store your credentials in this demo. Voice recording, consent, council rules and access control all work locally without any government API key.

Once approved, a future version could add translation or speech-to-text as an optional backend service. Audio must never be sent to external services unless speaker and council permissions explicitly allow that use.
